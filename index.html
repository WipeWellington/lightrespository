Bluetooth log (for troubleshooting)

Close the Govee app first (swipe it away). A lamp can only talk to one app at a time. Stay within about 30 ft. When the list pops up, pick the one with the lamp's model in its name (H6022 or H6076). It may start with Govee_ or ihoment_.

Lamp troubleshooting

If a lamp connects but colors don't change, open its Fix-it settings and try format B, then C. If max brightness looks dim, switch the brightness range.
